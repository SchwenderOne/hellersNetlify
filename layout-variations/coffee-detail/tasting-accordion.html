Die Geschichte

Hendra Maulizar

Gayo Avatara • Indonesia

Hendra Maulizar has been a driving force in Indonesian specialty coffee processing, continuously pushing boundaries to improve quality and elevate the country's coffee industry. His journey began in 2006 when his father, Drs. Hamdan, moved to Aceh and started growing coffee.

What started as a modest 1-hectare farm has since grown into a 10-hectare estate, producing some of the finest specialty coffee in Indonesia. His farm now boasts a fully equipped wet mill, gravitational washing channels, and raised drying beds.

Beyond his own farm, Hendra has built a strong network of 70 local farming families who partner with him to supply high-quality cherries for processing.
